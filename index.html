02 · Safety

It cannot cook your Mac.

Fan control makes people nervous, and it should. QuellHeat is built so its failure modes are boring.

  • macOS thermal safety is never overridden. Apple's own protections stay in charge underneath everything QuellHeat does.
  • Every write is clamped to your hardware's own limits. QuellHeat cannot command a speed your fans don't support.
  • If anything goes wrong, the fans hand straight back to Apple automatic. Quit it, crash it, let the licence lapse: the fans revert and monitoring keeps running.
  • Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. No terminal, no security bypass, no "right-click to open".

06 · Questions

Before you buy.

Do I have to pay to see my temperatures?

No. Download it and every sensor reading, the menu-bar readout, alerts and history are yours free, forever. The licence unlocks control of the fans.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple Silicon or Intel. On macOS 26 the interface uses true Liquid Glass; on earlier systems it falls back to native materials. Fan control needs a Mac that has controllable fans: a fanless MacBook Air will show sensors only.

Is it safe to control my fans?

QuellHeat never overrides macOS thermal safety, clamps every write to the limits your hardware reports, and hands the fans back to Apple's automatic control if anything goes wrong at all, including quitting the app, a crash, or a lapsed licence. Temperature monitoring keeps running in every one of those cases.

You get a licence key by email and paste it into the Activate window once per Mac. It works on two, and a 14-day offline grace period means travelling never locks you out. Updates arrive in the app itself, cryptographically signed and verified, and are included with your licence.
