02 · Safety

It cannot cook your Mac.

Fan control makes people nervous, and it should. QuellHeat is built so its failure modes are boring.

  • macOS thermal safety is never overridden. Apple's own protections stay in charge underneath everything QuellHeat does.
  • Every write is clamped to your hardware's own limits. QuellHeat cannot command a speed your fans don't support.
  • If anything goes wrong, the fans hand straight back to Apple automatic. Quit it, crash it, let the licence lapse — the fans revert, and monitoring keeps running.
  • Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. No terminal, no security bypass, no "right-click to open".

06 · Questions

Before you buy.

Do I have to pay to see my temperatures?

No. Download it and every sensor reading, the menu-bar readout, alerts and history are yours free, forever. The licence unlocks control of the fans.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple Silicon or Intel. On macOS 26 the interface uses true Liquid Glass; on earlier systems it falls back to native materials. Fan control needs a Mac that has controllable fans: a fanless MacBook Air will show sensors only.

Is it safe to control my fans?

QuellHeat never overrides macOS thermal safety, clamps every write to the limits your hardware reports, and hands the fans back to Apple's automatic control if anything goes wrong at all, including quitting the app, a crash, or a lapsed licence. Temperature monitoring keeps running in every one of those cases.

What are custom modes?

A custom mode is a one-click preset you build yourself: a name, an icon, a trigger sensor from Hottest and CPU Core Average to a sensor you name yourself, and a display sensor of your choice. Its speed is set as either a minimum, so fans never drop below it but still ramp up if it gets hot, or fixed, holding one number outright. Switch modes from the Modes tab or right from the menu bar. A fixed mode still yields to the overheat safety boost, so it can't cook your Mac either.

Can I change how it looks?

Yes. Settings ▸ Appearance offers the native macOS window material — the default, and what you get if you change nothing — plus eleven photographic backdrops. Each ships graded twice, once for light appearance and once for dark, so the window follows your Mac's setting rather than forcing one. Accent colour is yours to pick too. Warm colours are reserved for temperature and never used for anything else, so an amber reading always means heat.

How does activation work?

You get a licence key by email. Paste it into the Activate window once per Mac. It works on two Macs, and there's a 14-day offline grace period so travelling never locks you out.

How do updates work?

QuellHeat checks for updates itself and installs them in place, cryptographically signed and verified, so you never have to re-download from a website. Updates are included with your licence.

What's next for QuellHeat?

It keeps getting new features. Updates are free and arrive in the app, no reinstalling needed. If you've paid for a licence, email us with what you want built next: that's how you get a vote.

Why does it ask for an admin password once?

Writing fan speeds needs a small privileged helper, which macOS quite rightly makes you approve. You approve it once, in System Settings, and never again. It only ever writes fan registers.
